en_ru.txt — English → Russian translations
------------------------------------------
Derived from the OpenRussian.org dictionary data
(https://github.com/Badestrand/russian-dictionary), by the OpenRussian team
and contributors, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made: the Russian→English entries were inverted into an
English→Russian word list, ranked by frequency, limited to three
translations per word, and filtered of vulgar words, by
tools/build_dictionaries.py. This derived file is itself available under
CC BY-SA 4.0. The data is provided as-is, without warranties.
